BibleProject · A short study

Love

Not a passing feeling but a loyal, self-giving commitment—God's faithful love toward his people, and the love he invites them to share.

▶ Watch the video
01

Consider the story

Think about how the Bible describes a love that stays, sacrifices, and keeps its promises.

1

God describes himself to Moses as abounding in steadfast love and faithfulness. Why is God's love defined by loyalty and commitment rather than by emotion alone?

2

Hosea is told to keep loving a wife who has been unfaithful, as a picture of God's love for Israel. What does this reveal about the depth and cost of faithful love?

3

Jesus says the whole law hangs on loving God and loving your neighbor. Why are these two loves inseparable?

4

The New Testament says we know love by seeing Jesus lay down his life. How does the cross define what love looks like?

02

Reflect on the message

Consider what it means to receive a love that does not let go, and to pass it on.

5

God's love is committed even when it is unreturned. Where do you find it hard to believe you are loved without earning it?

6

Love in Scripture is shown through action, patience, and sacrifice. Who needs that kind of love from you right now?

7

Jesus calls his followers to love even their enemies. What would one small step toward that look like in your life?

One thought to carry with you
God's love is not a mood that comes and goes. It is a faithful commitment that stays, gives itself away, and invites us to love the same way.
Keep exploring: Read Exodus 34:4–7, Hosea 11:1–9, Mark 12:28–34, 1 Corinthians 13:1–8, and 1 John 4:7–12. Notice how God's loyal love becomes the pattern for every kind of love.

For personal study or group discussion. The questions are open-ended; the aim is thoughtful understanding, not a single perfect answer.
