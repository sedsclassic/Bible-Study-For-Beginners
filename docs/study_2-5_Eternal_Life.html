Eternal Life

Eternal life is more than living forever. Jesus invites us into God's life now—and promises a future in which that life will be complete.

▶ Watch the video
01

Understanding eternal life

Eternal life is something people can receive and experience. What does this life mean, and where does it begin?

1

How is eternal life different from simply existing forever? What does the phrase suggest about the quality of life God offers?

2

In John 17:3, Jesus describes eternal life as knowing the Father and Jesus Christ. What does it mean to truly know God?

3

Why does Jesus speak about eternal life as something we can have now, rather than only something we receive after death?

4

How does Jesus make it possible for people to share in God's life? Why are his death and resurrection central to this promise?

02

Reflect on the invitation

Eternal life changes how we understand the present, the future, and our relationship with God.

5

If eternal life begins now, what might change in the way we live our ordinary days?

6

We often look for a full life through achievement, comfort, or recognition. How might Jesus' understanding of life challenge those expectations?

7

How can the promise of a future with God give us hope when life is painful, uncertain, or incomplete?

One thought to carry with you
Eternal life is not merely a future destination. Through Jesus, we are invited to know God and participate in his life today, while looking forward to the day when that life will be fully realized.
Keep exploring: Read John 3:16–17, John 5:24, John 10:10, John 17:1–5, 1 John 5:11–12, and Revelation 21:1–5. Consider how eternal life begins through knowing God and points toward the restoration of all things.

For personal study or group discussion. The questions are open-ended; the aim is thoughtful understanding, not a single perfect answer.
