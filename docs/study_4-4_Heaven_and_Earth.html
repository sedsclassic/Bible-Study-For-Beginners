BibleProject · A short study

Heaven and Earth

Two realms made to belong together—from Eden and the temple to Jesus, and the new creation where God's presence fills the earth.

▶ Watch the video
01

Consider the story

Think about how the Bible keeps returning to places where God's space and human space meet.

1

The Bible opens with God creating the heavens and the earth. What does it suggest that its very first story is about two realms made to belong together?

2

Eden is pictured as a place where God's space and human space overlap. What does this reveal about what God originally desired for humanity?

3

The tabernacle and temple are built as small places where heaven and earth meet again. Why does the story keep returning to sacred spaces where God dwells among his people?

4

The New Testament presents Jesus as the one in whom heaven and earth meet. How do his resurrection and ascension point toward their final reunion?

02

Reflect on the message

Consider what it means to live as people who belong to both heaven and earth.

5

Jesus taught his followers to pray for God's will to be done on earth as in heaven. Where might you invite heaven's kind of life into an ordinary place this week?

6

If the Bible's hope is a renewed earth rather than an escape from it, how might that change the way you treat creation and your daily work?

7

Where do you sense God's presence breaking into ordinary moments? What might help you notice it more often?

One thought to carry with you
The Bible's hope is not escaping earth for heaven, but heaven and earth joined at last—with God living among his people in a world made new.
Keep exploring: Read Genesis 1:1, Genesis 28:10–17, Exodus 25:8–9, Matthew 6:9–13, and Revelation 21:1–5. Notice how God's presence moves from a garden, to a tent, to a person, and finally to the whole renewed creation.

For personal study or group discussion. The questions are open-ended; the aim is thoughtful understanding, not a single perfect answer.
