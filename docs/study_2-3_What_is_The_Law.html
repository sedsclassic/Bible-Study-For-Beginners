What is the Law?

More than a list of rules: how God's instructions teach his people to live with justice, love, and wisdom.

▶ Watch the video
01

Consider the story

Think about why God gives the Law and what it reveals about his relationship with Israel.

1

God gives the Law after rescuing Israel from slavery in Egypt. Why is it important that the relationship comes before the commandments?

2

The Law includes instructions about worship, relationships, justice, and everyday life. What does this suggest about how God wants his people to live?

3

The commandments reveal God's concern for both love of him and treatment of others. How are these two responsibilities connected?

4

Israel repeatedly fails to live according to God's instructions. What does this reveal about the difference between knowing what is good and actually doing it?

02

Reflect on the message

Consider what the Law reveals about God's character and what it means for our lives today.

5

If the Law reflects God's character, how might studying it help us understand what God values beyond simply learning what is permitted or forbidden?

6

Jesus says that he came not to abolish the Law but to fulfill it. How does understanding the purpose of the Law help us understand what Jesus came to accomplish?

7

It is possible to follow rules outwardly without having a heart shaped by love. How can we avoid treating faith as mere rule-following?

One thought to carry with you
God's Law was given to a people he had already rescued. It shows them how to reflect his character through love, justice, and faithfulness, revealing that God's desire is not simply obedience to rules, but a people whose lives are shaped by him.
Keep exploring: Read Exodus 19:3–6, Exodus 20:1–17, Leviticus 19:1–18, and Matthew 5:17–20. Notice how God's rescue comes before the Law, and how Jesus brings its purpose into focus.

For personal study or group discussion. The questions are open-ended; the aim is thoughtful understanding, not a single perfect answer.
