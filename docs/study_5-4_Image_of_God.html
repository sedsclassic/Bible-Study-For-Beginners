BibleProject · A short study

Image of God

Humanity made to reflect God's character and rule in the world—distorted by sin, and restored in Jesus, the true image.

▶ Watch the video
01

Consider the story

Think about what it means that humans, rather than statues or temples, are called God's image.

1

In the ancient world, kings set up images of themselves to mark their rule, and temples housed images of the gods. What is Genesis saying by declaring that every human being is God's image?

2

Humans are made to rule and care for creation as God's representatives. What does this reveal about the purpose and dignity of every person?

3

After Eden, humanity's image-bearing is cracked, and people begin to reflect violence, pride, and idolatry. How does the Bible's story explain this distortion without erasing our original dignity?

4

The New Testament calls Jesus the true image of God and says believers are being renewed into it. How does he show what humanity was always meant to be?

02

Reflect on the message

Consider how bearing God's image shapes the way you see yourself and others.

5

If every person bears God's image, whom are you most tempted to overlook or devalue? How would seeing that person as God's image change how you treat them?

6

You were made to reflect God's character in the world. Where do you sense that image shining through you, and where does it feel dim?

7

Jesus is described as the pattern of true humanity. What is one way his life could reshape the person you are becoming?

One thought to carry with you
You are not an accident or a mere animal. You are made as God's image, and in Jesus that image is being restored in you.
Keep exploring: Read Genesis 1:26–28, Psalm 8, Colossians 1:15–20, Romans 8:28–30, and Colossians 3:9–11. Notice how the image humanity was given becomes the image humanity is renewed into.

For personal study or group discussion. The questions are open-ended; the aim is thoughtful understanding, not a single perfect answer.
