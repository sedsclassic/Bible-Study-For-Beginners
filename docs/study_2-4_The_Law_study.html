Purpose of the Law

From commandments to love: understanding God's purpose for the Law and what it means for followers of Jesus today.

▶ Watch the video
01

Consider the story

Think about who received the Law, why it was given, and how Jesus brought its purpose into focus.

1

The Law was given to Israel as part of its covenant with God, not as a universal legal code for every nation. Why does understanding its original audience matter when we read it today?

2

God wanted Israel to become a distinct people who would bring blessing to the nations. How were the commandments meant to help Israel fulfill this purpose?

3

Jesus challenged interpretations of the Law that focused on outward behavior while neglecting the heart. What does this reveal about the difference between obeying a command and understanding its purpose?

4

Jesus summarized the Law through two commands: love God and love your neighbor. How do these two forms of love bring together the purpose of the entire Law?

02

Reflect on the message

Consider how the Law's wisdom can shape our understanding of faith, freedom, and love.

5

If Christians are not required to follow every command given specifically to ancient Israel, why should we still study those commandments? What can they teach us about God's character?

6

Religious rules can sometimes become more important to us than the people they were meant to serve. Where might this happen in our own lives or communities?

7

Paul teaches that Christians should consider one another's consciences when dealing with disagreements. How can love guide us when believers understand certain practices differently?

One thought to carry with you
God gave the Law to shape Israel into a people who reflected his character and brought blessing to the world. Jesus reveals its deepest purpose: love for God and love for others. The goal was never simply to follow rules, but to become people whose hearts reflect God's goodness.
Keep exploring: Read Deuteronomy 6:4–9, Matthew 22:34–40, Matthew 23:23–28, and Romans 14:1–13. Notice how love, the purpose of the Law, and consideration for others connect throughout these passages.

For personal study or group discussion. The questions are open-ended; the aim is thoughtful understanding, not a single perfect answer.
