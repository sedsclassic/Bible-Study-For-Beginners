BibleProject · A short study

The Messiah

The promised deliverer—from the first promise in Eden to Jesus' victory over evil and death.

▶ Watch the video
01

Consider the story

Think about how God's promise develops throughout the Bible and comes to life through Jesus.

1

What promise does God make after humanity's rebellion in Eden? Why does this promise shape the rest of the biblical story?

2

Why aren't David and his descendants able to fulfill God's promise of a perfect king? What kind of Messiah does humanity truly need?

3

Isaiah describes a king who suffers for the evil of others. How does this challenge our usual understanding of power and victory?

4

How does Jesus fulfill the promises made throughout the Bible? Why is his resurrection essential to his victory over evil and death?

02

Reflect on the message

Consider what Jesus' victory means for the way we understand and live our lives.

5

If evil affects both the world and our own hearts, where do you see its influence in everyday life? How can Jesus help us confront it?

6

Jesus defeats evil through sacrifice rather than domination. What does this reveal about God's character, and how might it change the way we treat others?

7

Jesus promises a future where evil and death will be gone. How might this hope change the way you face today's struggles?

One thought to carry with you
Jesus is not simply a king who defeats his enemies. He takes humanity's evil upon himself, overcomes death through resurrection, and opens the way toward the restoration of creation.
Keep exploring: Read Genesis 3:14–15, 2 Samuel 7:11–16, Isaiah 52:13–53:12, and Revelation 21:1–5. Notice how God's promise of a deliverer develops and finds its fulfillment in Jesus.

For personal study or group discussion. The questions are open-ended; the aim is thoughtful understanding, not a single perfect answer.
